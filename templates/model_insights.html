🎓 Cybersecurity Concept: Precision vs Recall

In Phishing Detection, Accuracy alone is misleading due to class distributions and safety tradeoffs.

  • Precision (FP control): Measures what percentage of URLs flagged as phishing were truly malicious. Low precision causes legitimate business domains to be blocked.
  • Recall (FN control): Measures what percentage of actual phishing URLs were caught by the model. Low recall lets phishing attacks bypass detection into user inboxes!
